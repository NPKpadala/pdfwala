Split PDF

Break one PDF into separate pages or sections - pull out just the pages you need and download them in a tidy ZIP.

What Split PDF does

Split PDF separates a single PDF into individual pages (or ranges) and returns them as a ZIP. It is the reverse of merging: instead of joining files you break one apart, so you can share, re-order or delete pages independently without editing the original.

Why you need it

Often you only need one page of a long document - a single invoice from a statement, one signed page from a contract, or a chapter from a book. Splitting lets you extract exactly what you need instead of sending a 200-page file.

How to split pdf in 3 steps

  1. Upload. Add the PDF you want to split.
  2. Split. Click Split PDF and we separate the pages on our servers.
  3. Download. Get a ZIP with your pages, each as its own PDF - auto-deleted within 2 hours.
