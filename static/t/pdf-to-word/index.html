PDF to Word

Turn a PDF into a fully editable Word document - keep the text, tables and layout, then edit it like any .docx.

What PDF to Word does

PDF to Word converts a PDF into a Microsoft Word .docx file. It rebuilds the text, paragraphs, tables and basic layout so you can open the result in Word, Google Docs or LibreOffice and edit it directly - no retyping. It works best on PDFs that already contain real text; for scanned pages, run OCR first to make them editable.

Why you need it

PDFs are made for viewing, not editing. When you need to update a contract, reuse the text of a report, or fix a typo in a document someone sent you, converting to Word saves hours of copying and reformatting by hand.

How to pdf to word in 3 steps

  1. Upload. Add the PDF you want to convert.
  2. Convert. Click Convert to Word - larger files are processed in the background.
  3. Download. Download the editable .docx and open it in Word or Google Docs.

Frequently asked questions

Will the formatting be perfect?

For PDFs with real text, layout, paragraphs and tables are reconstructed closely. Very complex designs may need small touch-ups in Word.

Does it work on scanned PDFs?

Scanned pages are images, so convert them with our OCR PDF tool first to create a text layer, then convert to Word.

What format is the output?

A standard .docx file that opens in Microsoft Word, Google Docs and LibreOffice.

Is my document kept private?

Yes. It is processed automatically, never shared, and deleted within 2 hours.
