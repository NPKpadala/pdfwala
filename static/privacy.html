Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 July 2026

PDFWala ("we", "us") operates the online PDF tools at pdf.npkpadala.com. We built this service to be privacy-friendly: you do not need an account, and we keep as little data as possible. This policy explains what happens to your files and information.

The short version: your files are uploaded only to process the task you asked for, are processed automatically on our server, are never shared or sold, are never used to train any model, are not viewed by a human, and are automatically deleted within 2 hours.

1. Files you upload

  • Purpose: a file you upload is used solely to perform the operation you requested (compress, convert, merge, OCR, etc.) and to let you download the result.
  • Automatic deletion: uploaded files and generated outputs are stored temporarily on our server and are automatically deleted within 2 hours. We do not keep long-term copies.
  • No human review: processing is fully automated. We do not open, read, or manually inspect your documents.
  • No training / no sharing: your files are never used to train machine-learning models and are never sold or shared with third parties for their own purposes.
  • Processing location: files are processed on our own server infrastructure, not sent to third-party document services.

2. Information we collect

  • No account: we do not ask you to register, and we do not collect your name, address, or similar personal details.
  • Server logs: like almost all websites, our servers and our network provider record standard technical data (IP address, browser type, timestamp, requested URL) for security, abuse prevention, and rate limiting. We also keep an anonymous count of unique visitors. This data is not used to identify you personally.

3. Cookies and advertising

We use only the cookies needed to run the site and protect it from abuse. We may display advertising provided by third parties such as Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, and learn more at Google's advertising policies.

4. Third-party services

  • Cloudflare - content delivery, TLS, and security (bot/DDoS protection). Cloudflare may process request metadata on our behalf.
  • Google AdSense - advertising, where shown (see above).

5. Data security

Traffic is encrypted in transit with HTTPS/TLS. Access to our servers is restricted, and temporary files are purged automatically. No online service can be guaranteed 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

6. Children

PDFWala is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your choices

Because we do not require an account and delete files automatically, there is little personal data to manage. You can stop using the service at any time, clear cookies in your browser, and opt out of personalized ads as described above.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.
