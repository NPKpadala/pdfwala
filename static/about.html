About PDFWala

PDFWala is a fast, privacy-friendly suite of online tools for working with PDF and document files - compress, convert, merge, split, OCR, sign, protect, and a real in-browser editor, with 50+ tools in total.

It is built to respect your privacy: no account is required, your files are processed automatically on our own servers, and they are deleted within 2 hours. Nothing is shared, sold, or used to train any model.

Why we built it

Most document tasks should be quick, free, and private. PDFWala aims to do exactly that - do the job in your browser flow, without sign-ups or hidden handling of your documents.
